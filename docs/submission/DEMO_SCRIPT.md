# SortLocal: demo video script (2:30)

**Setup before recording**
- Open the deployed site in a clean browser profile (or clear site data) so the first-visit tour appears.
- Browser width about 1280 px, zoom 110%, light theme. Have a phone-width window ready for shot 9.
- Screen-record at 1080p. Record the narration separately and keep it calm and unhurried.
- Total target: 2:30 (the hard limit is 3:00).

| # | Time | Screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:12 | Title card: "SortLocal: recycling rules, by city". Then cut to a photo or B-roll of three different recycling bins. | "Is this plastic bag recyclable? In Toronto, yes. In Seattle, no, it goes in the garbage. In San Francisco, neither: take it back to the store." |
| 2 | 0:12–0:25 | Still on the B-roll, overlay the text "33 of 82 shared items → different bins". | "Of the 82 items all three cities cover, 33 go in a different bin depending on the city. That's why generic sorting advice, including generic AI, contaminates recycling." |
| 3 | 0:25–0:42 | The site loads and the tour appears. Click through the 3 steps; on step 1 click **Seattle**. Final button: **Show me the plastic bag**. | "SortLocal turns each city's official guide into rules as code. A 3-step tour gets you going. I'll pick Seattle." |
| 4 | 0:42–1:02 | The Compare page opens on the plastic grocery bag: banner "3 cities, 3 different answers". Hover each column's quote and click one source link (it opens SPU in a new tab), then come back. | "Same item, three answers, and each one carries a verbatim quote and a link to the city's own source. We don't paraphrase. A test checks every quote word for word against our archived copy of the official guide." |
| 5 | 1:02–1:15 | Scroll to **Where the cities disagree most**. Click **Disposable diaper** (Toronto: Green Bin). | "Here's every disagreement, ranked. Diapers go in the compost in Toronto and the garbage everywhere else." |
| 6 | 1:15–1:35 | **Look up** tab. Type "cardbord" (typo) and select **Cardboard box**: Recycling cart. Tick "It's greasy, wet or has food on it" and watch it switch to the Food & yard waste cart with a new quote. Show the "Same item, other cities" panel. | "Look-up handles typos and synonyms. You get the bin, the prep steps, and the 'it depends' cases, like a greasy box going to compost instead. If a city's guide doesn't cover something, SortLocal says so instead of guessing." |
| 7 | 1:35–2:05 | **Bin audit** tab. Click **Load example bin**: big number 48%. Point at the fix list (batteries flagged as a safety issue first, then the bagged glass and the dirty tubs). Point at **The same bin in each city**: SF 45%, Seattle 48%, Toronto 14%. | "Now measure it. I enter what's in my recycling bin. Under Seattle's rules it's 48% contaminated by weight. SortLocal ranks the fixes: batteries first because they start fires, then bagged recyclables and food residue. Each fix shows the rule behind it. And the exact same bin would score 14% in Toronto." |
| 8 | 2:05–2:20 | Click **Save audit**, then **Apply all fixes** (0%), then **Save audit** again. The trend chart shows 48% → 0% and the estimated CO₂e avoided. | "Save it, make the fixes, save again, and you get a before-and-after trend plus an estimate of CO₂e avoided, using EPA WARM factors and clearly labeled as an estimate." |
| 9 | 2:20–2:30 | Quick cuts: the **Rules & sources** page with the unverified list, the phone-width layout in dark mode, then the end card with the URL and GitHub. | "Every source and every unverified rule is listed. It runs as a static site with no account and no API key. SortLocal: recycle right, locally." |

**B-roll / overlay text ideas:** "Rules as code"; "282 cited rules"; "No AI guesses: official quotes"; "Contamination by weight + kg CO₂e (est.)".

**Don'ts:** don't claim exact real-world CO₂ savings. Say "estimated". Don't show an API key. Don't rush shot 7, because it is the core impact moment.
